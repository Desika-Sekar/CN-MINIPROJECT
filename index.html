    How to read this

    • Blue-grey shapes are the bank locations. HO is the big sphere, DC is the cylinder, ATM is the box.
    • Grey lines are the leased lines. The number at the middle of a line is its cost.
    • Blue packets are distance vector messages: neighbours swapping their whole tables.
    • Orange packets are link state packets: one router's own links, flooded to everybody.
    • A green, thicker line is a highlighted route.
    • A red line is a failed leased line.

    A router flashes green and a caption appears next to it whenever its table changes. Costs are in "routing units"; a cost of 24 is shown as unreachable. In distance vector a round counts only when something changed; in link state a step counts for every flooding hop plus the Dijkstra pass. That difference is written on the comparison table.
